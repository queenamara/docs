Bridge connectors link your existing tools — project managers, email, calendar, CRM, and more — to the AI in your Command Center. Once connected, you can ask Bridge to do real work across all of them in a single conversation. No switching tabs, no copy-pasting between apps.

<Note>
  **Free plan:** connect up to 3 tools. **Paid plans:** unlimited connectors. Upgrade anytime from your account settings.
</Note>

## How to connect a tool

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connectors page">
    Go to [bridge-mcp-967806289134.us-central1.run.app/connectors](https://bridge-mcp-967806289134.us-central1.run.app/connectors) in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Find the tool you want and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Complete the OAuth or API key flow for that tool. Bridge stores your credentials securely — you only do this once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using it">
    Return to your Command Center and start asking Bridge to take action.
  </Step>
</Steps>
